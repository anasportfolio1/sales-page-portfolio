How these differ from last time

Dot sizes here run 1 to 4px with spacing around 14 to 22px. The earlier version used much larger dots much further apart, which is what made it read as blobs rather than texture.

Only the panel under your pointer is drawing. On the real page the effect would sit behind the content on a single full width canvas, pausing whenever the tab is hidden.

All six respect prefers-reduced-motion and switch off for anyone who asks their system to reduce animation.
