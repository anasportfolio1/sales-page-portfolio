The part that eats the week

The shoot takes two hours. The rest takes the week.

Running a real estate media business is mostly not photography. It is follow ups, order chasing, file checking, delivery links, and the outreach you keep meaning to get to.

The inbox never closes

Booking requests, revision notes and agent questions arrive all day. Every one of them interrupts an edit you were halfway through.

Delivery is a second job

Check the files, organise the folders, upload, send the link, then remember which orders are still outstanding and which editor owes you a fix.

Outreach keeps slipping

New work needs prospecting. Prospecting needs hours. The hours go to the orders already booked, so next month looks like last month.

What we handle

Five jobs. One partner.

Take one service or all five. Scope, responsibilities and communication are agreed with you before anything starts.

Outreach

Social media, email and phone prospecting aimed at real estate agents who book media.

  • Identify and research relevant agent prospects
  • Build and organise prospect lists
  • Personalised messages based on each prospect's business
  • Follow ups on the agreed sequence
  • Manage replies and flag interested prospects

Media quality control

Completed property media checked against your standards before it reaches your client.

  • Exposure, brightness, colour and white balance
  • Alignment, vertical lines and presentation
  • Visible editing errors and inconsistencies
  • File completeness against the order
  • Flag images needing correction, coordinate revisions

Media delivery

The daily admin of getting finished work to the client, off your desk.

  • Organise completed property orders
  • Confirm required files are present before sending
  • Upload through your delivery platform
  • Check order details and delivery links
  • Send notifications and track what is outstanding

Content and personal branding

A consistent professional presence that shows the work you are already doing.

  • Content planning, posts and carousels
  • Captions and educational content ideas
  • Content from recent property shoots
  • Profile optimisation and brand messaging
  • Scheduling and publishing approved content

Admin and operations

The recurring work that fills a day without moving the business forward.

  • Inbox triage, flagging and approved replies
  • Business calls, messages and routing
  • Booking enquiries and order updates
  • Editor coordination and deadline follow up
  • Records, spreadsheets and task lists

AI photo QC handling

We review AI generated QC flags, check questionable images by hand, and coordinate corrections. Automated checks and human review are two different things and we treat them that way.
