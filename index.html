Breakdown

A welcome flow, decision by decision.

Anyone can show you a nice looking email. Here is the thinking underneath one, so you can judge whether I know what I am doing.

The brief

Curious, but suspicious

A meal replacement brand. The new subscriber is interested but the real objection is never price. It is "will this actually taste like food". A discount code does nothing for that.

The structure

Five emails, one job each

Email one earns the belief. Every email after it removes exactly one reason to not order. No discount anywhere in the flow.

The objection

Answered before it is asked

The taste worry gets met with a money back promise instead of an adjective. A promise is checkable. A claim is not.

The proof

A number, then borrowed authority

Four million people, stated plainly, then press logos. The reader has not heard of the brand yet, so the flow lends it credibility it has not earned in their head.

The design

Built to be skimmed

Every section is one idea with one image and one button. Thumb scrolls, eye lands on the button, nothing competes with it.
